## Products & Services

### Yacht Insurance

Policies tailored to a yacht's **hull value** and the **liabilities** an owner
may encounter while cruising locally or globally. Coverage is matched to the
vessel type and its intended navigation area.

### Charter Boat Insurance

Coverage for vessels carrying passengers for hire. Because maritime law places
extraordinary responsibility on the master of a fare-paying passenger vessel,
charter policies address the elevated liability exposures these operations
carry.

### Commercial Marine Insurance

Packaged policies for marine businesses that bundle the necessary coverages
into a single, cost-effective policy — designed to protect a variety of marine
operations against their specific exposures.

### Protection & Indemnity (P&I) / Crew Liability

If a yacht owner hires a paid captain or crew, those individuals must be
**specifically named in the P&I policy** to be covered against injury. Some
policies include incidental crew liability; many do not — a key detail to
confirm when a vessel is professionally crewed.

## Proprietary Programs

When adequate market coverage did not exist, Novamar built its own programs:

- **Mexican Vessel Liability Program** — Satisfies Mexico's requirement that
  boaters in Mexican territorial waters carry liability insurance from a
  Mexico-domiciled company. Offered at lower premiums with broader coverage
  than typical alternatives; serviced through the Puerto Vallarta office.
- **Novamar Offshore Program** — Coverage tuned for owners cruising offshore /
  globally.
- **Novamar Yacht Program** — Coverage for owners cruising locally.

## Quick FAQ

**Does Novamar cover boats cruising in Mexico?**
Yes. Its Mexican Vessel Liability Program is built specifically to meet Mexico's
requirement for locally-domiciled liability coverage, serviced from the bilingual
Puerto Vallarta office.

**Is a paid captain automatically covered?**
No. A paid captain and/or crew must be specifically named in the P&I policy to
be covered for injury. Confirm crew coverage before relying on it.

**What waters can be insured?**
U.S. inland and coastal, the Caribbean, the North/South Atlantic and Pacific,
and the Mediterranean — subject to the vessel and navigation limits on the
specific policy.
